# Product brief — AIKON Invest Lab

## Problem
A beginning Brazilian investor needs financial education, trustworthy portfolio records, BRL/USD currency tracking, and risk explanations in a single private dashboard.

## MVP
- Personal simulated portfolio: configurable portfolios, instruments, trades, cash movements, fees, prices, currency conversion, allocation and goals.
- Manual entry and CSV import with preview and deduplication.
- Deterministic holdings and performance calculations, separating contributions from investment return.
- Market data interface with explicit provenance, timestamp, market calendar and stale-data warnings.
- Educational AI assistant with citations, no trade execution, and privacy-aware data access.
- Portfolio overview with risks (concentration, FX exposure, drawdown once historical series exists).

## Out of scope for MVP
Real brokerage execution, financial advice automation, options leverage, copy trading, tax-filing automation, price prediction, paid real-time exchange feeds.

## Risks
Incorrect FX conversion, corporate actions, stale quotes, survivorship bias, foreign tax rules, inappropriate recommendations, data leakage, API license violations.
